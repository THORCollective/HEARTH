## Why
- DLL side-loading allows malicious code to execute under the identity of a legitimate, digitally signed application, bypassing application allowlisting and reducing suspicion from both users and security tooling.
- In this campaign, the technique provided the initial foothold for executing shellcode that ultimately loaded CNCMachineRMS, a fully featured RAT with remote desktop control, surveillance, and payload execution capabilities, making detection at this stage critical to disrupting the infection chain before persistence and C2 are established.
- This behavior is a recurring and deliberately preserved technique across the ROMULUS delivery platform, observed in multiple infection chains (Stardock, Canon, and others), and shares architecture with the separate BabaDeda/CNCMachineRMS infection chain, indicating it is a consistent tradecraft choice likely to persist as ROMULUS and similar delivery platforms evolve.
