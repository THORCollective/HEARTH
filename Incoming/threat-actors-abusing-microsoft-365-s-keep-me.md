## Why
- APT42's abuse of the KMSI feature after successful MFA push-bombing represents a stealthy persistence mechanism that blends entirely into legitimate Microsoft 365 authentication flows, making it extremely difficult to distinguish from normal user behavior using standard alerting.
- Successful exploitation of this technique grants the adversary long-term, re-authentication-free access to victim mailboxes, OneDrive, and SharePoint content without needing to repeatedly solicit MFA approvals from the victim, reducing the chance of detection or victim suspicion.
- This technique is directly tied to APT42's broader cloud operations lifecycle, in which credential harvesting and MFA bypass (via fake DUO pages, SMS token capture, and push fatigue) are immediate precursors to covert data exfiltration from Microsoft 365 environments, including the creation of app passwords for additional persistent access.
