## Why
- This hunt addresses compromised Okta credentials that are highly likely to have been intercepted by an AitM toolkit. 
- If activity is validated by tracing provenance of the activity to a source of either a malicious SMS or email, then the victim's cloud identity can be considered fully compromised and in need of containment and eradication.
- This hunt connects to a re-emerging campaign that Okta Threat Intelligence has previously reported on.
- This hunt would be valuable to the community because it utilizes intelligence that is timely and relevant to any English-speaking organization given this activity seems opportunistic and does not target a specific vertical. This hunt has also been validated in a live enterprise environment and successfully yielded true-positive findings.

## Implementation Notes
Required Data Sources: Okta Audit Logs
SPL Query:
```
index=okta sourcetype="OktaIM2:log"
| rename request.ipChain{}.ipDetails.ipServicesCategories{}.operator as ip operator
| stats count dc(displayMessage) as dc_msg values(displayMessage) as msg 
          values(outcome.reason) as outcome values(ip_operator) as ip_operator 
          values(published) as published values(client.ipAddress) as src_ip 
  by actor.displayName authenticationContext.rootSessionId client.ipAddress
| eventstats dc(client.ipAddress) as dc_ip 
                   dc(ip_operator) as dc_ip_operator 
                   values(ip_operator) as all_ip_operators 
  by actor.displayName
| where dc_msg >= 4
        ``` Change to "dc_ip >= 2" if your org doesn't already use a VPN/Proxy service ```
        AND dc_ip >= 3
        AND dc_ip_operator > 1
        AND like(displayMessage, "%Enroll new device%")
| fields published dc_ip src_ip actor.displayName dc_ip_operator all_ip_operators client.ipAddress authenticationContext.rootSessionId dc_msg count displayMessage outcome
```
