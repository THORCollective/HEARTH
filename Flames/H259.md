# H259

Threat actors are performing adversary-in-the-middle proxy attacks that relay legitimate Microsoft 365 OAuth authentication flows through attacker-controlled reverse proxy infrastructure to intercept session tokens and authorization codes after MFA completion, enabling reuse of stolen sessions from rotating residential proxy IP addresses.

## Why
- AiTM proxy toolkits defeat traditional MFA by relaying the genuine authentication flow in real time, intercepting session tokens and authorization codes after the challenge is satisfied rather than harvesting static credentials — making them invisible to controls that only validate MFA completion.
- Successful token theft grants the actor a valid, MFA-satisfied session that can be replayed indefinitely (or until revoked), enabling stealthy reconnaissance and mailbox collection without triggering credential-based alerts, MFA re-registration prompts, or password change notifications.
- This activity directly enabled the "Payroll Pirates" (Storm-2755) campaign's ability to compromise hundreds of Microsoft 365 tenants across healthcare, education, manufacturing, and government sectors, using rotating residential proxies to disguise session reuse as legitimate consumer traffic and evade geolocation/impossible-travel detections.
